NCM 107: THE INFANT (Birth Through 12 Months) 
MARIA FE L. TABUJARA RN, MAN 
 
1 MONTH 
A. Weight:  
❑ gains about 150 to 210 g (5 to 7 oz) weekly during 
the first 6 months of life  
❑ DOUBLE BIRTH WEIGHT AT 6 Months, TRIPLE 
WEIGHT at 12 Months 
 
B. Height:  
❑ grows about 2.5 cm (1 inch) a month for the first 
6 months of life  
❑ Health growth should not be erratic (up and 
down) but consistent  
❑ Physiologic loss 5-10% 0f BW (6-10 oz)  
❑ Increase by 50 % in 1st year (46-54 cm)  
❑ NB recaptures weight within 10 days 
 
• Anterior Fontanel: closes at 12-18 M (diamond shape)  
• Posterior fontanel: closes at birth to 2 M (TRIANGLE)  
• Anterior fontanel: bulging sign of increased ICP, 
depressed DHN  
• HT, WT, HC should be appropriately located on the 50th 
percentile, any deviation of which may be seen as a 
problem.

10 TO 12 MONTHS 
 
PHYSICAL 
 
Weight: birth-weight triples 
Height: birth-length increases by 50% 
❑ Head and chest circumference are equal 
❑ Upper and lower lateral incisors usually have 
erupted, for total of 6 to 8 teeth 
 
SOCIALIZATION AND VOCALIZATION 
• Shows emotions such as jealousy, affection, anger 
• Enjoys familiar surroundings and will explore away from 
mother 
• Fearful in strange situation or with strangers' clings to 
mother “stranger anxiety” 
• May develop habit of "security" blanket 
• Can say two words besides Dada or Mama with meaning. 
understands simple verbal requests, such as, "Give it to 
me." 
 
NUTRITION 
Breastfeeding – is the most desirable complete diet for 
the infant during the first 6 months of life. 
Solid foods – at 6 months 
✓ GI tract has matured sufficiently to handle more 
complex nutrients and less sensitive to allergenic 
foods.  
✓ Extrusion reflex has disappeared 
✓ Head control is well developed 
 
SOLID FOODS 
❑ Iron-fortified cereals –preventing iron deficiency 
anemia. Least allergenic and most easily digested 
food. 
❑ Vegetables 
❑ Fruits 
❑ Meat and eggs 
❑ Egg yolk at 6 mos. Egg white at 9-10 mos. 
❑ avoid giving honey and corn syrup 
 
• Proportion size is 1 tbsp per year of age. 
 
PARENTAL CONCERNS 
• Teething 
• Thumb sucking 
• Sleep concerns 
• Constipation 
• Loose stools 
• Colic – paroxysmal abdominal pain
