NCM 107: PRE-SCHOOL 
MARIA FE L. TABUJARA RN, MAN 
PRESCHOOLER (3 TO 5 YEARS) 
A. PHYSICAL TASKS 
1. Slower growth rate continues 
a) Weight: increases 4-6lb (1.8-2.7kg) a year 
b) Height: increases 2-3.5 (6-8cm) 
c) 
Birth length doubled by 4 years 
 
2. Vital signs decrease slightly 
a) Pulse 85/min 
b) Respirations: 24-25/min 
c) 
BP: systolic 100/60 mmHg 
 
3. Gross motor development 
a) walks upstairs using alternate feet by 3 years. 
b) walks downstairs using alternate feet by 4 years. 
c) 
rides tricycles by 3 years. 
d) hops on 1 foot by 4 years. 
e) skips and hops on alternate feet by 5 years. 
f) 
balances on 1 foot with eyes closed by 5 years.  
g) throws and catches ball by 5 years. 
h) jumps off 1 step by 3 years.  
i) 
jumps rope by 5 years. 
 
4. Fine motor development 
a) hand dominance is established by 5 years. 
b) builds a tower of blocks by 3 years. 
c) 
ties shoes by 5 years. 
d) ability to draw changes over this time 
✓ copies circle, may add facial features by 3 
years. 
✓ copies a square, traces a diamond by 4 
years. 
✓ copies a diamond and triangle, prints 
letters and numbers by 5 years 
e) handle scissors well by 5 years. 
 
B. PYSCHOSOCIAL TASKS (INITIATIVE VS GUILT) 
1. Becomes independent 
❑ feeds self completely 
❑ dresses self 
❑ takes increased responsibility for actions. 
 
2. Aggressiveness and impatience peak at 4 years 
then abate; by 5 years the child is eager to please and 
manners become more evident. 
 
3. Gender-specific behavior is evident by 5 yrs. 
4. Egocentricity changes to awareness of others; 
rules become more important; understands sharing 
 
 
 
 
 
 
C. COGNITIVE DEVELOPMENT 
1. Focuses on one idea at a time; cannot look at entire 
perspective. 
 
2. Awareness of racial and sexual differences begins. 
❑ prejudice may develop based on values of parents 
❑ manifest sexual curiosity 
❑ sexual education begins 
❑ beginning body awareness. 
 
3. Understanding of time develops 
 
4. Egocentrism – perceiving that one’s thoughts and 
needs are better or more important than those of others. 
 
D. SAFETY 
1. Safety issues similar to toddler 
 
2. Education of children concerning potential dangers 
is important during this period. 
 
3. Car safety: children 20-40lb and younger than 
age 4 should ride in car safety seat. Children over 
40lb and between ages 4 and 8 should ride in a 
booster seat in the rear of the car. 
 
E. PLAY 
1. Predominantly “associative play” period. 
 
2. Enjoys imitative and dramatic play. 
o 
imitates same – sex role functions in play. 
o 
enjoys dressing up, dollhouses, doctor and nurses 
kit. 
3. Provide toys to help develop gross motor skills: 
tricycles, wagons 
 
4. Provide toys to encourage fine motor skills, self-
expression, and cognitive development 
 
5. Imaginary playmates common during this period. 
 
F. FEARS 
 
1. Greatest number of imagined and real fears of 
childhood during this period. 
 
2. Fears concerning body integrity are common. 
❑ observing injuries or pain in others can precipitate 
fear. 
❑ magical and animistic thinking allows children to 
develop many illogical fears (fear of inanimate 
objects, the dark ghosts) 
 
3. Exposing child to feared object in a safe situation 
may provide a degree of conditioning

• OEDIPUS COMPLEX – refers to the strong emotional 
attachment a preschool boy demonstrates toward his 
mother. (in short mama’s boy) 
 
• ELECTRA COMPLEX – refers to the attachment of a 
preschool girl to her father. (in short daddy’s girl)
